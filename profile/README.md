# PECathon 2026

PECathon is the flagship hackathon organized by **PEC ACM**, the ACM Student Chapter at Punjab Engineering College, Chandigarh, as part of **PECFEST**, the institute's annual technical festival.

PECathon 2026 is a **17-hour, offline hackathon**. It challenges participants to think creatively, collaborate effectively, and develop practical solutions under time constraints.

Whether you're passionate about AI/ML, web development, backend systems, or emerging technologies, PECathon provides a platform to experiment, innovate, and turn concepts into tangible projects.

## About PEC ACM

**PEC ACM** is the ACM Student Chapter at Punjab Engineering College, Chandigarh, and a community of 300+ Computer Science enthusiasts working to advance computing as a science and profession.

Our mission is to strengthen the coding culture at PEC, foster technical excellence, and create opportunities for students to learn, build, and collaborate. As part of the global Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) network, the chapter connects students with a broader community of computing professionals, resources, initiatives, and opportunities.

Through technical events, collaborative projects, competitions, and community initiatives, PEC ACM strives to cultivate a strong culture of innovation and continuous learning.

PECathon reflects this mission by bringing students together to solve problems, experiment with technology, and turn ideas into working solutions.
